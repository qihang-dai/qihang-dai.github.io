Kagerō

A Sekiro-style deflect duel that runs in the browser.

His attack strings are random every time, and each deflect is judged from your key press's timestamp, not rounded to the nearest frame. Break his posture, then land a deathblow.

Pixel JRPG · Boss fight

Pale Hour

A reactive turn-based fight against the Hourkeeper.

Pick commands on your turn; on its turn, parry, dodge and jump its strikes in real time. Parry a whole string and you counter. Three phases, and a twelve-strike finale at midnight.

Play
  • Pixel art drawn in code
  • Chiptune synthesized live
  • 3 difficulties

ControlsJ parry · K dodge · Space jump

Greystep

The same party, facing the Basalt Warden.

An over-the-shoulder turn-based boss fight in the style of Expedition 33. Every model is generated in code; at half health the Warden's seams burn red and it gains the five-hit Cataclysm.
